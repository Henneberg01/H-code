## Official name and compatibility

The official product name is **JFH-CODE**, formerly H-Code. See [JFH-CODE-PRINCIPLES.md](JFH-CODE-PRINCIPLES.md) and [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md).

Existing identifiers such as `.hc`, `.hcs`, `hcodec`, `hcode.lock.json`, the internal Rust library name `hcode_compiler` and `HCODE_*` remain supported to preserve compatibility.
